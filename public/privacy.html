Privacy Policy

HSH Contractor (HSH Drywall) · Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how HSH Contractor (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our contracting services and the HSH GC Platform (the “App”).

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and profile data (name, role) when you sign in.
  • Contact information: the mobile phone numbers of crew, subcontractors, customers, and general contractors who provide them for job communication.
  • App data: projects, estimates, actuals, change orders, documents, and related data created in the App.
  • QuickBooks data: if you connect QuickBooks, OAuth tokens used to read/write data in your QuickBooks company as permitted by your authorization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to operate our services and the App, coordinate and communicate about jobs, sync with QuickBooks when you choose, improve the service, and comply with law. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Text Messaging (SMS) Program

HSH Contractor (HSH Drywall) sends text messages for scheduling and job-related communication to crew members, subcontractors, customers, and general contractors who provide their mobile number and consent to receive them. Consent is obtained directly — in person during employment or subcontractor onboarding, or when a customer engages HSH for a project — and is documented in writing on HSH’s onboarding form or work agreement. We do not use a public web form to collect opt-in, and consent to receive texts is not a condition of employment, subcontracting, or any purchase.

At sign-up, each person is presented with and agrees to the following: “I agree to receive operational text messages from HSH Drywall about job scheduling, material delivery, and project coordination at the mobile number I provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.”

Message types include job schedule updates, appointment and delivery coordination, and job-status notifications. Message frequency varies based on job activity.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to deliver the messages described above; message delivery is handled by our SMS provider (Twilio).

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time, or reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact us at the details below with any questions.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers (including Supabase) and, when you use QuickBooks, transmitted to Intuit’s QuickBooks Online API under their practices. We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

6. Third Parties

Supabase hosts our database and authentication. Twilio delivers our text messages. Intuit / QuickBooks: when you connect QuickBooks, Intuit’s OAuth and API policies apply to that data. We do not share your data with other third parties for marketing.

7. Your Choices

You can reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time, disconnect QuickBooks from the App settings, and request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.
